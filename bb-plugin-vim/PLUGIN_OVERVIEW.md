Vim brings vim-style keys to all of bb, so you can work without the mouse.
Press `j` and `k` to move through the sidebar's threads, menus, Library
results, and PR Board cards, or to scroll a chat. Press `h` and `l` to move
between the sidebar, the chat, split panes, the side panel, and board columns.

Press `f` to label every button and link on screen with letters, then type
the letters to click it. `i` jumps to the composer, `/` to search, `gt` and
`gT` switch threads, and `?` lists every key. While you type in a text field
or work in a terminal, every key goes there; `Esc` brings you back.
