Spotify in bb, with a mini player you can open anywhere with ⌘⇧S.

## What you get

- **Playback control** for the Spotify app on this Mac or any of your devices:
  playlists, Liked Songs, search, seek, volume, and save.
- **Smart shuffle** that spreads out artists, favors songs you haven't heard
  lately, drops songs you usually skip, or follows a vibe you describe, ordered
  by your local `claude` CLI.
- **Sharing**: copy a song link or URI, make a share card, or @-mention the song
  in a thread so the agent gets its details.
- **Agent-aware music** that lowers the volume or pauses while a thread waits
  for your approval.
- **Listening history and stats**: top artists and songs, listening by hour,
  skip rate, and playlists you've forgotten.
- **Launcher results**: find and play Spotify playlists and songs from ⌘K.
- **Eight looks**: Console, Receiver, Liner Notes, Sticker Book, Voxel, Neon,
  Concept Sketch, and Wabi-sabi, each with its own palettes.

## How it works

You register a free Spotify app and paste its Client ID; sign-in uses PKCE with
no secret. Spotify requires Premium for its developer mode. Tokens, history,
and caches stay in this plugin's database on the bb server.
