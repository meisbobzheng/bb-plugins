Thread Sidebar replaces bb's sidebar thread list with one in the style of T3
Code. Threads are grouped by project in roomy rows that show what each one is
doing, its branch, and how long ago it changed. Threads waiting on your answer
or approval are tinted amber, listed first in their project, and counted on
the project's header, so you can see at a glance where you are needed. Pinned
threads stay on top, collapsed projects still show the threads that need you,
and every row has bb's full thread menu.
